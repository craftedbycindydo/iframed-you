Retention and curation rules

ChoiceRule
Last 7, 30, 90 or 180 daysUses the email received date, with a separate capacity of 5–50 shown photos.
Last 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50 photosNewest eligible, unique, non-excluded photos regardless of age. Excluded pictures do not consume these slots.
Trash / excludeHides that file from the gallery, Overview and all slideshows. Its private ID stays excluded across Check now, pruning, expiry and restart.
Undo / restoreUndo restores a cached photo immediately. Local picture choices in Settings can restore one or all exclusions. If the cache copy is gone, Check now can import it again.
FavouritesFavourites-only slideshows and favourite preference on Overview remain available. They affect presentation, not storage retention.
Storage boundariesAt most 50 cached images altogether, including any excluded copies kept for Undo. Exclusion hashes are separate and bounded to 4,096. Explicit cache erase, disconnect or account change clears them.

The default stays at 30 photos from 30 days. Checks read the inbox without changing mail, scan at most 1,000 messages and 100 image parts, apply any exact-sender rule, deduplicate the original file bytes and cache resized JPEGs without EXIF data. Changing the file bytes makes a new photo ID. A large inbox may yield fewer photos than the requested count. Lowering retention removes older cached files; expanding it requires Check now to refill.

All 33 screenshots are synthetic; physical captures stay private. Click an image for full resolution. The shared Google album continues to use Google’s player; these curation rules apply to the emailed-photo gallery.
