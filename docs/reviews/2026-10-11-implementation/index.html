Design changes by screen

Across the app

Use the available landscape area instead of scaling one fixed canvas. Core month/Overview surfaces stay within the page, while long agendas, libraries and Settings can scroll where needed. Keep large touch targets, visible focus, bounded information density and optional controls behind a clear action. Preserve warm palettes, astronomical automatic mode, reduced motion and Momo.

Settings grew to eight categories because Household and Services now have their own preferences. The everyday rail stays familiar; unused Home/YouTube can be hidden. New Household navigation and the Overview note require an explicit opt-in. No widget builder or extra always-visible dashboard panels were added.

Overview · A balanced, adaptive dashboard

Fluid landscape layout and three bounded presets. Labelled Forecast and Calendar routes, large clickable weather/event surfaces, a safe gap beneath photos, today-anchored plans, contextual Calendar setup and an optional one-line household note. Healthy timestamps stay in Settings.

Weather · A compact forecast

Shorter current-condition hero, readable forecast rows, sunrise in the supporting note and a Change city route to the shared editor. Saved forecast and retry states remain explicit. All nine existing scenes retain day/night variants.

Photos · local library · A full-width photo library

The library uses the available width. Photo settings opens the shared category with a return route. Coarse saved/checking/checked status, slideshow and manual check remain visible; favourites and reversible hide choices survive refresh/reload.

Photos · shared album · A compact shared-album launcher

Source switching remains in Photos. Album and idle configuration share the Settings editor. The page identifies Google’s player separately from Skylit’s email-photo styles and offline cache.

Photo browser, zoom and all slideshow styles · Photo options only when needed

Browse and Slideshow labels distinguish modes. An Options drawer holds favourite, hide, Fit/Fill, local caption and received date. Closing restores thumbnail focus; keyboard focus stays in the player. Shuffle, repeat and favourites-only are optional.

Photo delivery, retention and disconnect · Presentation, Delivery and Storage

Broad presentation/delivery groups, a collapsed account form with empty credential fields, import-verification guidance and a separate storage/erase area. Bounded polling, exact-address trusted senders and progress counts use native private storage.

Calendar · month, agenda and event details · Month, Week and Today without page scrolling

Management is grouped under Calendar options. Pending inviter review appears only when needed. Source chips toggle visibility; search opens on demand and covers loaded dates. The month grid fits the landscape canvas and the agenda scrolls internally.

Calendar · management and connection help · A shared editor with a return route

Back to Calendar, connected labels and refresh state precede the add form. Labels/colours change without revealing or re-entering a feed URL. Removal remains confirmed; private-link instructions use the agreed read-only integration.

Calendar · inviter review · Decisions before setup

Pending review comes first, approved inviters next, and owner/policy setup lives in a disclosure. Exact-address Show/Hide on frame decisions update Calendar and Overview. Loaded-date and read-only limits remain explicit.

Home controls · A compact external-service panel

One primary launch action, an owner verification checklist and an optional navigation toggle. The UI asks the owner to confirm a real device operation; it does not infer authenticated controls.

YouTube · Layout beside launch

Mobile/standard layout and launch live together. Up to six owner-entered public video/playlist shortcuts are optional; both JavaScript and native code validate exact HTTPS destinations. External playback/return still needs frame acceptance.

Settings · appearance, Momo and device controls · Eight focused categories

Appearance, Momo, Weather & time, Photos, Calendars, Household, Services and Device & status. One existing editor is reused per preference. Larger text, live appearance preview and six light/dark pairs remain independent. Recovery actions and advanced device controls stay here. Saving feedback reserves its space.

First-run and reopened setup guide · Configured and verified are distinct

The guide distinguishes credentials/configuration from a received picture or refreshed feed, remembers its current step, and offers Continue setup from the shared editors. Existing-install launch suppression remains covered by tests.
